# FMSuperScout 1.7.3

**No more scouting players who are already gone.** Players who agreed a move to another club
(say, joining Barcelona on 1 July) now show it, and stay out of your way.

- **Agreed transfers, straight from FM**: the club a player is joining and the date. You see
  it in the club column ("Mallorca → Barcelona"), as a tag in the status column and on the
  player card.
- **Left out by default.** Your search no longer fills up with players who are already sold.
  Tick "Incl. agreed transfers" to see them anyway. Players from, to or owned by your own club
  always stay visible, with the label.
- Only real agreements count: open bids and rejected offers are ignored.

Checked on Windows against a running FM 27.0 save. The Mac version gets the same changes but
was not re-tested on a Mac for this release.

FMSuperScout only reads; it never changes Football Manager, Steam or your saves.
