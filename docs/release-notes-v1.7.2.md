# FMSuperScout 1.7.2

**My club, per squad.** A small fix with a big effect on your own squad view.

- **Loaned-out players under the right squad.** A reserve or U18 player out on loan used to
  show up under "First team", because the app looked at the team he plays in at the loan club.
  It now uses the squad he belongs to at your club, read straight from FM.
- **Your club opens on the first team.** Click your club at the top and you get the first team
  only, including players you loaned out from it. Reserves and youth are one click away on the
  squad chips. The filter label says which squad is on.

Checked on Windows against a running FM 27.0 save. The Mac version gets the same changes but
was not re-tested on a Mac for this release.

FMSuperScout only reads; it never changes Football Manager, Steam or your saves.
